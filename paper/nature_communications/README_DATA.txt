MSO raw ROS 2 recordings for manuscript verification

This package contains the raw ROS 2 recordings supporting the bounded
physical analyses reported in the manuscript:

- 10 core two-robot recordings;
- 8 auxiliary controlled single-robot recordings;
- 18 SQLite bag files and 18 associated metadata.yaml files (36 files);
- 4,438,635,447 bytes in total, comprising 4,438,380,544 bag bytes and
  254,903 metadata bytes (approximately 4.134 GiB).

The files are provided for verification of the manuscript evidence. Exact
relative paths, byte sizes and SHA-256 values are listed in
RAW_ROSBAG_SHA256SUMS.md.

No separate data licence is granted with this package. Providing access does
not alter any third-party rights that may apply to the recordings or their
contents.
