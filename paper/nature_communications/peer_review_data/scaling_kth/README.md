# Team-size and KTH retained records

The A2 files are one retained aggregate coverage trace for each team size. The
original experiment record identifies five configured seeds per condition, but
run-level seed traces were not retained. The first recorded steps with coverage
at least 0.5/0.8 are 30/79, 21/82 and 12/24 for teams of 2, 3 and 5 robots,
respectively. Their last retained steps are 387, 221 and 96, with recorded
endpoint coverages 0.838528, 0.834842 and 0.942750. These unequal trace
horizons preclude a matched-horizon scaling claim.

The A3 files contain retained aggregate coverage traces for one KTH scene. The
first recorded 0.5/0.8/0.9 crossings are 7/33/36 for nearest frontier,
8/24/45 for full MSO and 7/39/44 for MSO without merge. Their recorded
endpoint coverages are 0.953555, 0.950919 and 0.952126, respectively. The
historical occupied-cell-precision series
was not retained in machine-readable form and is not displayed in the revised
supplementary figure. These files therefore support descriptive coverage
statements only, not seed-level uncertainty or a quantitative
occupied-cell-precision range.
