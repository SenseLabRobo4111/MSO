# Proposed manuscript-review access terms

**AUTHOR APPROVAL REQUIRED.** These proposed terms do not take effect until the
corresponding author confirms that all authors and relevant institutions are
authorised to grant them. Until that confirmation, the repository carries no
general licence beyond the separate upstream terms listed in
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and rights supplied by applicable law or the hosting
service.

## Intended permission after approval

For editors and invited peer reviewers evaluating the manuscript *Design and
Audit of Resource-Constrained Predictive Multirobot Exploration*, the
authors intend to permit retrieval, transient local copying, execution and
modification of the authors' repository material solely as reasonably necessary
to assess the manuscript. This limited permission would end when the review and
appeal process ends.

It would not grant permission to redistribute the repository, publish modified
versions, use the material commercially, train unrelated models, or treat the
recovered checkpoints as validated manuscript artifacts. Third-party material
would remain governed by the licences identified in
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## Public and controlled material

The Git repository is public, so its contents are not confidential merely by
being hosted there. Any large raw dataset delivered through a controlled
reviewer link is different: it should be used only for editorial and peer-review
evaluation, stored securely, not redistributed, and deleted after the review
and appeal process unless the journal requires longer retention.

No controlled raw-data link is present in this repository. Access details must
be entered through the journal's secure submission system after the author has
verified the uploaded files, checksums, permissions, expiry date and reviewer
instructions.

## No warranty

The research material is supplied for evaluation as is, without warranty or a
claim that every historical experiment can be reproduced. The README and
component-specific provenance documents define the known evidence boundaries.
