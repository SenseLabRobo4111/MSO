- The registered benchmark contains 12 method names.
- Eleven methods completed five seeds each (55 completed runs).
- RePaint remains at 0/5 and is not represented as a completed comparison.
- Seed-level results, method summaries, completion status and two-sided 95%
  t intervals are retained under `results_uniform/aggregate/`.
- The corresponding SVG, PDF, PNG and 600 dpi TIFF are retained under
  `results_uniform/final_visualizations/` with a byte-level manifest.
- The figure uses all completed seeds, performs no smoothing, and does not
  select a best seed.

The uniform-objective comparison is deliberately architecture-focused and is
not evidence that MSO is the best-performing method. It
does not implement the full MSO teacher, adversarial, perceptual, pixel and
distillation objective. Under this common objective, MSO showed substantially
larger seed-to-seed variability than the other completed methods. This is the
reason for the separate full-objective experiment below; it is not a basis for
discarding any MSO seed.

## Evidence boundary

This is a prospective reconstruction of the paper equations, not a recovery
of the lost historical trainer or the historical manuscript checkpoint. The
current split is a sample-hash split (6,142 train / 699 validation / 1,463
test), not the unrecovered 5,385/1,356 split and not a verified
building-disjoint split. Results therefore support a within-archive benchmark,
not building-level generalisation.

A separate graphics process was sharing the GPU during training. This did not
interrupt optimisation, but latency and resource numbers must be remeasured on
an otherwise idle GPU or explicitly labelled as shared-GPU measurements.
