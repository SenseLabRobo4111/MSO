## Evidence boundary

This is a prospective reconstruction of the paper equations, not a recovery
of the lost historical trainer or the historical manuscript checkpoint. The
current split is a sample-hash split (6,142 train / 699 validation / 1,463
test), not the unrecovered 5,385/1,356 split and not a verified
building-disjoint split. Results therefore support a within-archive benchmark,
not building-level generalisation.

A separate graphics process was sharing the GPU during training. This did not
interrupt optimisation, but latency and resource numbers must be remeasured on
an otherwise idle GPU or explicitly labelled as shared-GPU measurements.
